## Problem
Financial engineering teams do not just build software, they prove it.  
Most proof today is still manual:

- Scattered logs, screenshots, and "trust me" notes
- Inconsistent verification steps across teams
- Expensive reruns and environment rituals
- Incident response with missing or non-standard evidence

## Solution
Runs are the missing primitive.  
Continuum creates a single, shareable Run record that includes:

- Plan, execution timeline, and outcomes
- Structured verification results
- Evidence bundle (manifest + artifacts)
- Policy gates (what must be true before shipping)

## How It Works
1. **Ingest** from Jira, workflow YAML, or CI trigger
2. **Plan** a deterministic step graph (serial + parallel)
3. **Execute** through plugins (lifecycle, transport, verification)
4. **Prove** with tamper-evident evidence bundles
5. **Publish** reports back to Jira, CI, or internal portals

## Key Capabilities
- Deterministic runs: resume, retry, selective execution
- Evidence-first artifacts: per-step outputs + hashed manifest
- Policy gates: "can't ship unless evidence exists"
- Trace correlation: API tests, logs, and DB checks tied together
- Integrations: Jira, GitHub/GitLab, CI, Postman/Newman, Splunk/ELK, Mongo/SQL

## FAQ
### Is this replacing CI/CD?
No. Continuum complements pipelines by orchestrating real operational workflows and producing evidence receipts.

### Is it FedNow-specific?
No. FedNow is a wedge. Continuum is rail-agnostic and fits any regulated workflow.

### How do you handle security?
Secrets stay out of evidence bundles. Evidence is tamper-evident and policy-gated.
